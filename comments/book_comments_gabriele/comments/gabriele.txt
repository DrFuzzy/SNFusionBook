Hi Themis! Here are my own comments on Chapters 4 to 8. I recorded them as voice notes while reading the printed book, so below they are transcribed and lightly cleaned up. Page numbers are those of the same version (10–11 Nov); where I refer to a passage I quote it so it's easy to find. I didn't check the exercises yet.

*General remarks*

- Chapter 4 reads more like the state of the art of a paper than a tutorial. Chapter 3 was very clear: toy examples, how to do things; you read it and you understand how to implement the methods. In Chapter 4 I often don't see what the reader is learning.
- Throughout Chapter 4 we present particular cases (specific sensors, range and visibility, OctoMap, IMU preintegration) as if they were general methodologies. I would first give the general approach and then the application.
- There is a lot of forward referencing throughout the chapters.
- Some symbols are heavily overloaded (σ, K, k, F, α).
- Chapter 5 is the natural place for all the autonomous-driving details that are now in Chapter 4. One way to restructure would be to move some of the Chapter 4 content there, and keep in Chapter 4 the generality of the methods and what is useful for the reader.
- I would remove Chapter 7 altogether (see below).
