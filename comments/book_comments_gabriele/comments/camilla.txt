Hi Themis! Camilla also went through the PDF of the book and left comments directly on it. She did not write an overall message, so below are her comments one by one, translated from Italian. Page numbers are those of the version she annotated (10 Nov, the same one Valeria used); I quote the highlighted text so it's easy to find. Her comments stop at p. 140 (Chapter 6).

*Recurring points*

- Several passages read like a paper rather than a book (chapter abstracts, "Scope & Contributions", the organisation of the book described twice).
- In Chapter 4 the subsections are short and self-contained: she often cannot see why a method is presented, what it is for, how it connects to the previous subsection and to autonomous driving.
- Some claims rest on a single reference or on none.
- The generic algorithms (Algorithms 1, 2, 3) are lists of sentences and add little.
- Sentences built as "vague statement: explanation" sound chat-like; she would remove them as far as possible.

*Comments one by one*

Are you leaving it like this? It seems a bit too vague.

Is this standard in a book? It looks very much like a paper, and so does the section after it, "Motivation".
