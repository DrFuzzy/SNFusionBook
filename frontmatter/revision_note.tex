\chapter*{Note on the Revision Markup}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Note on the Revision Markup}
\noindent This working copy shows the changes of the October 2026 revision round. All earlier rounds have been accepted and appear in black. In this round, \added{text in blue has been added} and \deleted{text in red, struck through, has been removed}; longer removed passages appear in red without strike-through. Chapter and section titles follow the outline agreed with the publisher; new material appears as subsections, worked examples, chapter abstracts, and end-of-chapter problems whose solutions are collected at the end of the book. Every numerical example can be reproduced with the scripts and notebooks of the companion repository (\repopath{companion\_\allowbreak repo/\allowbreak }), but the book compiles without it. Setting \texttt{\textbackslash revisionmarkupfalse} in \texttt{main.tex} produces the clean version.
