# Chapter 6 — Sensor Fusion in Multi-Agent Systems: problems

Problem statements of the end-of-chapter problems (same numbering as in the book). Solutions to the computational problems are in `solutions/`; the scripts are self-contained (numpy/scipy only) and deterministic.

1. **Convergence rate of consensus on a path.** Write the Laplacian of the path graph 1–2–3–4 and verify that its eigenvalues are 0, 2−√2, 2, 2+√2. Compute the contraction factor ρ for ε = 0.3 and the number of rounds needed to reduce the initial disagreement by a factor of 100. Compute the optimal constant step ε* and ρ*, and explain why ε* violates the sufficient condition ε < 1/d_max without compromising convergence. *(analytical)*
2. **Average preservation on directed graphs.** Show that the Laplacian iteration preserves the average on undirected graphs and that the weighted iteration preserves it iff W is column-stochastic. For W = [[1/2,1/2,0],[1/3,1/3,1/3],[0,1/2,1/2]] and z(0) = [10,12,11], compute the left Perron eigenvector, the consensus value and compare with the average 11. *(analytical)*
3. **Covariance-intersection weight.** Scalar case P1 = 1, P2 = 4: find the weight minimising P_CI and interpret. Two-dimensional case P1 = diag(1,4), P2 = diag(4,1) with the trace criterion: find ω* and P_CI, compare with independent fusion. *(analytical)*
4. **Double counting of a common prior.** Two nodes share a scalar prior of variance 1 and each takes one measurement of variance 1. Compute the posterior variance of each node, the variance claimed by the naive sum of information, the centralised posterior variance, the actual error variance of the naive estimate and its expected NEES. *(analytical)*
5. **Consensus rounds and topology.** Rerun the consensus-on-information filter of the distributed-tracking example with L ∈ {1,3,10} on the ring-with-chord graph and on the path graph 1–2–3–4–5; report RMSE and mean NEES and explain the dependence on L via the second largest eigenvalue modulus of the Metropolis matrix. *(computational: `solutions/prob05_consensus_rounds.py`)*
6. **Packet loss.** Add i.i.d. message losses with probability p ∈ {0,0.3,0.6} to the consensus-on-information filter, with weights renormalised over the received messages; report RMSE and mean NEES and check consistency. *(computational: `solutions/prob06_packet_loss.py`)*
7. **Robustness of the path graph.** Show that the path graph is 1-robust but not 2-robust; run W-MSR with F = 1, ε = 0.3 on the path with agent 4 broadcasting 30 m and describe the behaviour of the compliant agents. *(analytical + computational: `solutions/prob07_wmsr_path.py`)*
